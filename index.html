01 — About Ram Interior

Experience. Professional Expertise. Skilled Execution.

“With more than 35 years of experience in the business, Ram Interior focuses on one thing — getting the work done, correctly and on the ground.”

Ram Interior is a premier Mumbai-based contractor and project execution company specializing in high-quality civil and interior work. Supported by a professional team of architects, interior designers, skilled workers, and trade specialists, we manage projects seamlessly from initial requirements and planning through to complete on-site execution. Our comprehensive services include civil work, interior fit-out, custom furniture, and all integrated electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work.

As a trusted turnkey project execution company in Mumbai, our 35+ years of experience and portfolio of 100+ completed projects speak for themselves. Our expertise shows up exactly where it matters most: in how a project is actually run. We excel at coordinating diverse trades, keeping work strictly on track, and paying meticulous attention to the fine details that a successful build depends on.
